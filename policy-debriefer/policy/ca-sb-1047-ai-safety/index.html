California SB 1047: Frontier AI Safety Bill

A California bill that would have required makers of the largest AI models to adopt safety plans and allow a shutdown option. The governor vetoed it.

Reading level

This bill targeted only the biggest, most expensive AI models. Companies would have had to write safety plans, test for serious dangers, and be able to switch the model off. It passed the legislature, but Governor Newsom vetoed it, saying it focused on model size instead of how risky the actual use is.

What changes

  • Safety plans for very large AI models
  • Shutdown capability
  • Whistleblower protections
  • Enforcement by state Attorney General

Each side is written the way its own supporters would make the case. Neither is endorsed. The middle column covers what is actually contested.

Case from the left

Reasonable guardrails before AI gets more powerful.

  • Sets basic safety expectations for the most capable systems.
  • Protects employees who flag dangers.
Neutral read

Supporters and opponents included people from both parties and inside the AI industry itself, making this a technology-policy debate more than a partisan one.

Open questions
  • Should AI rules be based on model size or on how the model is used?
Case from the right

Premature regulation that would hurt innovation and open-source.

  • Liability fears could chill open model releases.
  • Better handled federally than state-by-state.
