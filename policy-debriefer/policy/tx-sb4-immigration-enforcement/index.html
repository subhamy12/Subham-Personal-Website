Texas SB 4: State Illegal-Entry Law

A Texas law making illegal border crossing a state crime and letting state judges order people to return to Mexico. Federal courts have blocked it.

Reading level

Normally only the federal government enforces immigration law. This Texas law made crossing the border illegally a state crime, so local police could arrest people and state judges could order them to leave. Courts paused it because immigration is usually a federal job.

What changes

  • Illegal entry becomes a state crime
  • State and local police can arrest
  • State judges can order removal to Mexico

Texas stepping in where Washington failed.
