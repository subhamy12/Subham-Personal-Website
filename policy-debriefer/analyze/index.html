Not available in this preview

Analyzing a policy runs several AI passes on a server, so it’s turned off in this read-only preview. Every other part of the site works here: browse the debriefs, filters, the compass and following.

Tips for a better score
  • Primary sources (the bill text or a government page) score highest for evidence.
  • Congress.gov bill links are read through the official API automatically.
  • If a site blocks us, paste its text and keep the URL so it’s still cited.
  • Opinion pieces work, but they lower the evidence score.
What you’ll get
  • Plain-English summary at two reading levels
  • The case from the left and the right
  • Topic, focus and lean labels with confidence
  • Claims checked against the source
